## 👋 About me

I am a **Customer Success and Sales professional in tech** at [Eco Webdesign](https://www.ecowebdesign.com.br), in São José do Rio Preto, Brazil, since July 2025. I train customers and sell websites and online stores. I am also an **AI-assisted web developer**: I use **AI every day** to code, automate routines and document what I learn.

## 💼 What I do today

- 🎓 **Training and support:** I teach customers to manage their website, online store and news portal on [Interago](https://www.interago.com.br/site/index), Eco's platform, so they can run their own projects.
- 🤝 **Sales and partnerships:** I worked side by side with [Maria Brasileira](https://www.mariabrasileira.com.br/index) franchisees across Brazil, in the process that led to more than **110 landing pages** sold, and taught them how to use the control panel.
- 🤖 **AI and automation:** I develop with AI agents (such as Claude Code) and automate work routines.
- 🌐 **Business websites:** HTML, CSS and JavaScript, focused on responsive design and conversion.
